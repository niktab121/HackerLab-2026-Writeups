INTELLIGENCE PACKAGE 06 — LAST KNOWN POSITION

CONTEXT

Agent AVALD was transported from the Nordvelt Grand Hotel by a TSD
tactical unit on 2024-11-03. An operator involved in the transfer has
falsified the official transport records to conceal the destination.

A forensic evidence package was recovered from the operator's
workstation, including the transfer database, shell command history,
gate access logs, and operational documents.

Signals intelligence (SIGINT) stations across the Nordvelt region
monitored radio transmissions during the transfer window. The national
vehicle tracking system recorded GPS positions for fleet vehicles
throughout the day. Border and facility checkpoint passage records
are available.

Traffic camera footage from Route Nord-7 — the primary northern
transport corridor — was obtained under requisition.
