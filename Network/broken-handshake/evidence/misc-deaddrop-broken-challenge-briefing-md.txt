INTELLIGENCE PACKAGE 02 — BROKEN HANDSHAKE

CONTEXT

Agent AVALD's workstation established TLS connections to
relay-01.norveth.vld for secure communications with HQ. A network tap
at the relay site captured the TLS handshake sequences.

Preliminary analysis suggests a man-in-the-middle interception was
performed using a rogue certificate. Certificate Transparency logs
covering the relay domain and surrounding infrastructure were obtained
via legal subpoena. 510 log entries from multiple CT monitors are
included.

The Norveth Federation PKI trust anchors are provided for validation
purposes. The federation's TLS validation policy documents the
expected certificate verification behavior.

                              the operational environment

  trust_policy.md          Norveth Federation TLS validation policy
                           (certificate verification requirements)

                           interceptor certificate identification

OBJECTIVE

Identify the certificate used to intercept Agent AVALD's TLS
connection. Determine which organization issued it and attribute the
interception operation to the responsible party.
